FAQ

Common questions

Google Ads or Facebook ads for lead generation, which is better?

Google Search captures people already looking for your service, so leads are usually higher intent. Meta creates demand and often gives a lower cost per lead. Many businesses get the best results running both with clear tracking.

How do I improve lead quality from Facebook lead ads?

Use the higher-intent form type, add one or two qualifying questions, and send lead quality data back to Meta. Fast follow-up also makes a big difference.

What types of businesses do you run lead generation for?

Service businesses such as home services, education and visa consultancies, SaaS, water and utility services, real estate and B2B companies.

How quickly will I see leads?

Usually within the first week. The first 2–4 weeks are for learning what works; after that the focus moves to lowering cost per qualified lead.

Can you connect leads to my CRM or Google Sheets?

Yes. I set up lead syncing to Google Sheets or your CRM with automatic assignment to your sales team.
