FAQ

Common questions

How much should a Shopify store spend on Meta ads to start?

Enough to give each test meaningful data. For most stores that is at least $30–50 a day on Meta. Below that, results swing too much to judge which ads and audiences work. I'll recommend a budget based on your margins and average order value.

Do you work with small Shopify stores or only big brands?

Both. I have managed accounts spending up to $40K a day, and I also work with growing stores. What matters most is a product with healthy margins and a store that converts.

What is the difference between Advantage+ Shopping and regular campaigns?

Advantage+ Shopping lets Meta automate audiences and placements around your product catalogue. It often scales well for e-commerce, but it needs good tracking and strong creative. I usually run it alongside structured prospecting and retargeting tests.

Why are my Facebook ads not showing all my Shopify sales?

Usually because of browser tracking limits. Setting up the Conversions API with proper deduplication sends sales server-side, so Meta sees more conversions and optimises better.
