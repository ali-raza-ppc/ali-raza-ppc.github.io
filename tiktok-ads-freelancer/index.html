FAQ

Common questions

Is TikTok advertising worth it for e-commerce?

For many products, yes, especially visual, impulse-friendly or trend-driven products. TikTok works best when you can produce a steady flow of native-looking video creative.

How many creatives do I need for TikTok ads?

Plan for at least 5–10 new videos to start and a few fresh ones every week or two. Creative fatigue on TikTok is faster than on Meta.

What is a good CPA on TikTok ads?

It depends on your price and margins. I set a target CPA from your average order value and profit margin, then structure tests to beat it.

Do you make the videos?

I write creative briefs, hooks and scripts and can work with your creators or UGC providers. I can also repurpose your existing content into TikTok-native ads.

Can you manage TikTok and Meta together?

Yes. Most of my e-commerce clients run both. I share learnings between platforms so a winning angle on one gets tested on the other.
