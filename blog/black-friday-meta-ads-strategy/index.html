Black Friday Meta Ads Strategy for Shopify Stores

Ali RazaPerformance Marketing Specialist · 8 min read · Nov 2026

Black Friday and Cyber Monday (BFCM) are the most expensive and the most profitable days of the year for many Shopify stores. CPMs rise as every brand competes for attention, but buying intent rises faster. The stores that win usually prepare weeks ahead; the ones that lose launch new campaigns on the day and hope.

In 2026, Black Friday is 27 November and Cyber Monday is 30 November.

Quick summary

  • Warm up audiences 2–4 weeks before with content and early offers.
  • Use proven campaigns; add sale creative instead of launching from scratch.
  • Raise budgets gradually from the week before, peak on BF and CM.
  • Retarget hard during the sale, then re-engage new customers in December.

The BFCM timeline

WhenWhat to do
3–4 weeks beforeDecide the offer, check margins, brief creative, fix tracking
2–3 weeks beforeRun prospecting and engagement ads to grow warm audiences; collect emails with early access
Week beforeLaunch teaser and early-access ads; raise budgets gradually
Black Friday to Cyber MondaySale creative live, peak budgets, strong retargeting
After Cyber MondayExtend or wind down, then re-engage new customers for December

Get the offer and margins right first

A discount that wipes out your margin turns record revenue into a loss. Recalculate your break-even ROAS at the sale price. A 30% discount can push break-even ROAS up sharply, so decide your BFCM target before spending.

Offers that work well: tiered discounts (spend more, save more), bundles, free gifts above a threshold and early access for subscribers.

Campaign structure for BFCM

  • Keep proven campaigns running. Swap in sale creative rather than starting new ad sets that must go through learning.
  • Advantage+ Shopping for prospecting and existing customers together. See my setup guide.
  • Retargeting for site visitors, add-to-carts and engagers. See abandoned cart retargeting.
  • Catalog ads showing discounted products people viewed.

Budgets

Expect CPMs to rise during BFCM week. Plan your budget from your target CPA and expected volume, not last month's daily spend. Increase gradually in the week before, then raise again on Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Use campaign budgets and automated rules to protect against overspending if results fall below break-even. My guide on how much to spend on Facebook ads explains how to size it.

Creative that works during BFCM

  • The offer in the first second: "40% off everything, this weekend only"
  • Countdown and urgency, but only if the deadline is real
  • Gift angles: "for him", "for her", "under $50"
  • UGC with the offer added as text overlay
  • Bestsellers and bundles rather than the full catalogue

Prepare creative early; Meta's ad review can take longer in peak weeks.

After Cyber Monday

BFCM brings in many first-time customers. Retarget them in December with gift guides, bundles and shipping deadlines, and exclude recent purchasers from sale ads. This is where much of the BFCM profit is actually made.

Common mistakes

  • Launching brand-new campaigns on Black Friday morning
  • Not recalculating break-even ROAS at the discounted price
  • Waiting until the last week to brief creative
  • Stopping all ads on Cyber Monday night and losing December momentum

Frequently asked questions

When should I start Black Friday Facebook ads?

Start building audiences and testing creative two to four weeks before Black Friday, then increase spend in the days before and during the sale. In 2026, Black Friday is 27 November and Cyber Monday is 30 November.

Why are Facebook ads more expensive during Black Friday?

Many advertisers compete for the same users in the same week, so CPMs rise. Conversion rates also rise, so CPA can still be acceptable if your offer is strong and your audiences are warm.

Should I launch new campaigns on Black Friday?

It's safer to use campaigns and ad sets that have already exited learning, adding sale creative and increasing budgets, rather than launching everything new on the day.
