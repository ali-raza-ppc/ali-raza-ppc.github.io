Instagram Ads for E-commerce: Placements, Formats and Tips

Ali RazaPerformance Marketing Specialist · 8 min read · Nov 2026

For most e-commerce brands, Instagram is where customers discover products. Instagram ads run through Meta Ads Manager, the same place as Facebook ads, so the real skill is creating ads that feel native to each Instagram placement.

Quick summary

  • Instagram ads are created in Meta Ads Manager, usually alongside Facebook.
  • Main placements: Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore and profile feed.
  • Use 4:5 for Feed and 9:16 for Stories and Reels.
  • Native-looking video, UGC and catalog ads tend to work best for stores.

Ad formats that work for stores

  • Short video (15–30 seconds): show the product in use within the first seconds.
  • UGC: creator or customer videos feel like recommendations. My UGC brief guide shows how to get usable ones.
  • Carousel: several products, features or steps in one ad.
  • Catalog ads: automatically show products from your Shopify catalog to the right people. See my dynamic product ads guide.
  • Partnership ads: run ads from a creator's handle with their permission, which adds social proof.

Creative tips for Instagram

  1. Hook in the first second. Movement, a bold claim or the product in action. My ad hooks guide has formulas.
  2. Design for sound off. Add captions or on-screen text.
  3. Respect safe zones. In 9:16 ads, keep text away from the top and bottom where the profile name and buttons sit.
  4. Look native. Phone-shot video often beats polished studio work in Reels and Stories.
  5. Show the offer clearly near the end, with a simple call to action.

How to set up Instagram ads for e-commerce

  1. Connect your Instagram account and Facebook Page in Business Settings.
  2. Set up the pixel and Conversions API so purchases are tracked.
  3. Create a Sales campaign optimised for purchases.
  4. Use broad or Advantage+ audience and Advantage+ placements.
  5. Upload 4:5 and 9:16 versions of each ad so every placement looks right.
  6. Review the placement breakdown after enough spend before excluding anything.

Common mistakes

  • Running one square image across every placement
  • Putting key text where Instagram's interface covers it
  • Only boosting posts instead of building proper Sales campaigns
  • Excluding Facebook placements without testing

Frequently asked questions

Should I run Instagram ads separately from Facebook ads?

Usually not. Running both through Meta Ads Manager with Advantage+ placements lets Meta shift budget to whichever placements perform best. Separate Instagram-only campaigns make sense mainly for specific tests.

What size should Instagram ads be?

Use 4:5 vertical for Feed and 9:16 full-screen for Stories and Reels. Keep text and key visuals inside the safe zone, away from the top and bottom of 9:16 frames where interface elements appear.

Which Instagram placement is best for e-commerce?

It varies by brand. Reels and Stories often deliver reach at a lower cost, while Feed can convert well for considered purchases. Let Meta optimise across placements and review the placement breakdown before excluding any.
